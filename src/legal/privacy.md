This Privacy Policy explains how JR Capital and its affiliated companies, including R&R Grandeur Properties, JR Grand Homes LLC and JR Elite Rentals LLC ("JR Capital," "we," "us" or "our"), collect, use, share and protect personal information in connection with the website at jrcapitalgrp.com (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

## 1. Information we collect

### Information you provide

When you join the investor list or contact us, we collect what you give us. This may include:

- your first and last name;
- your email address and phone number;
- your typical investment range and the offerings you are most interested in;
- whether you have told us you are an accredited investor; and
- anything else you include in a message to us.

### Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, our hosting provider records standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed and the date and time of your visit. This information is used to operate and secure the Site.

## 2. How we use information

We use personal information:

- to add you to the investor list and tell you about offerings, if you asked us to;
- to answer your questions and communicate with you;
- to understand whether an offering may be suitable for you, and whether you may qualify for it;
- to operate, maintain, secure and improve the Site;
- to detect and prevent fraud, spam and abuse; and
- to comply with legal obligations and enforce our [Terms of Use](/legal/terms.html).

## 3. How we share information

**We do not sell or rent your personal information,** and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may share it only:

- with R&R Grandeur Properties, JR Grand Homes LLC, JR Elite Rentals LLC and other companies under common ownership with JR Capital, to respond to you and carry out our business;
- with service providers that help us run the Site and our business, who may use it only to provide their services to us;
- with attorneys, accountants, lenders and other professional advisers, where reasonably needed;
- when we believe disclosure is required by law, subpoena or court order, or is needed to protect the rights, property or safety of JR Capital or others;
- in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, sale of assets or similar transaction; and
- in any other case where you have asked or agreed to share it.

## 4. Forms, hosting and third-party services

The Site is hosted by Netlify, which also receives and stores what you send through the investor list form and delivers it to us. The Site's web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google and shares your IP address with it when a page loads. Our email provider handles the messages we exchange with you. Each provider handles information under its own privacy policy.

## 5. Cookies and tracking

The Site does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or third-party tracking pixels, and we do not track you across other websites. Our service providers may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies to deliver the Site securely.

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" setting. Because we do not track visitors across websites, the Site does not change its behavior in response to that signal.

## 6. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, such as keeping you on the investor list until you ask to be removed, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law, for example for tax, securities, accounting or record-keeping purposes. When we no longer need it, we delete it or make it anonymous.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections to the Site. No method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to:

- tell you what personal information we hold about you;
- correct information that is inaccurate;
- delete your information; or
- remove you from the investor list.

To make a request, email [raj.gumma@rrgp.io](mailto:raj.gumma@rrgp.io). We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy laws, and you will not be treated differently for exercising them.

## 9. Children's privacy

The Site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Other websites and visitors outside the United States

The Site may link to websites we do not operate. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

The Site is operated in the United States and is intended for visitors in the United States. If you visit from elsewhere, your information will be processed in the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your country.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
